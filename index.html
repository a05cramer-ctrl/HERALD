THE RULES

  • The post must include $HERALD and the code of the wallet that submits it.
  • The X account needs 50+ followers and to be 30+ days old.
  • Submit within 48 hours of posting. Each post can be submitted once.
  • Spam, bots and copy-paste get an AI score of 0. Identical texts only count once.
  • The 0.001 SOL submit fee goes into the pool and keeps spam out.
